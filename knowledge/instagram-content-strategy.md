# Instagram content strategy notes

## Useful content signals

Practical, specific content tends to earn saves when it gives the audience a checklist, process, or repeatable idea. Shares are a stronger signal of resonance than likes alone. Compare formats within the same account before applying general benchmarks.

## Experimentation

Change one variable at a time: format, opening hook, topic, or call to action. A seven-day plan should balance reach-oriented short video, save-oriented carousels, and conversation-oriented stories.

## Responsible recommendations

Use the account's own history as the source of truth. Avoid promising follower growth, inventing industry benchmarks, or treating correlation as causation.
